Saudi Arabia Garlic Import Market Overview

Saudi Arabia's arid climate and limited freshwater resources make large-scale garlic cultivation impractical, resulting in heavy reliance on imports to meet national consumption needs that exceed 150,000 tons annually. Garlic is a staple ingredient in Saudi cuisine, featured prominently in everyday cooking as well as in the elaborate feasts prepared for Ramadan, Eid celebrations, and large family gatherings. The Saudi market strongly favors Pure White Garlic in large sizes of 5.5cm and above, reflecting the premium positioning of the Kingdom's retail sector, where clean, bright white appearance and large bulb size command higher prices and consumer preference. Chinese garlic has become the dominant import source, prized for its large bulb sizes, consistent quality, and competitive pricing that helps importers manage costs in a price-conscious retail environment. Jeddah Islamic Port serves as the primary gateway for the western region, including the holy cities of Makkah and Madinah, while King Abdulaziz Port in Dammam handles imports for the eastern province. The Saudi Food and Drug Authority (SFDA) regulates all food imports, requiring compliance with strict food safety standards. Demand surges dramatically during Ramadan and Eid al-Fitr, when importers typically build inventory 2-3 months in advance. Shandong's cold storage infrastructure ensures year-round availability, enabling Saudi importers to secure stock ahead of these peak demand periods.
