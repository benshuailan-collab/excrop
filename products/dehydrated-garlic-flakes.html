Product Introduction

Dehydrated garlic flakes are thin, uniformly sliced garlic pieces that have been carefully dried to preserve their natural flavor, aroma, and nutritional value. They offer the convenience of garlic in a shelf-stable format — no refrigeration required, no peeling, and no waste.

Our flakes are produced from premium fresh garlic harvested in Shandong province. The raw garlic is cleaned, peeled, sliced to a uniform 1-2mm thickness, and dehydrated under controlled conditions to achieve a moisture content below 6%. The result is a clean, white-to-light-yellow product with consistent slice size and the full pungency of fresh garlic.

Dehydrated garlic flakes are essential ingredients for the food processing industry, seasoning manufacturers, instant food producers, and soup base makers. With a 24-month shelf life in sealed packaging, they provide supply chain flexibility that fresh garlic cannot match. ExCrop also supplies garlic granules and powder to meet diverse processing needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about dehydrated garlic flakes

What is the shelf life of dehydrated garlic flakes?
When stored in sealed moisture-proof packaging, away from light and moisture, dehydrated garlic flakes have a shelf life of 24 months. We use aluminum foil bags and sealed drums to maintain optimal freshness.
What forms of dehydrated garlic do you supply?
Our primary product is dehydrated garlic flakes (slices). We also supply garlic granules and garlic powder upon request. All forms are produced from the same high-quality raw garlic sourced from Shandong.
What is the minimum order quantity for dehydrated garlic flakes?
The minimum order quantity is 500kg. LCL (less than container load) shipping is available for dehydrated products, making it flexible for buyers who need smaller quantities.
Which industries use dehydrated garlic flakes?
Dehydrated garlic flakes are widely used in the food processing industry, seasoning manufacturing, instant food production, soup bases, and snack foods. They offer consistent flavor, long shelf life, and easy storage compared to fresh garlic.
