China Garlic Price per Ton – 2026 Buyer Questions

How is the China garlic price per ton calculated? Export prices are quoted FOB Qingdao in USD per metric ton (MT). One 40HQ reefer container holds about 28 MT, so the container price is roughly the per-ton price × 28 plus freight. The garlic price today depends on three factors: bulb size (5.0cm–6.5cm+), variety (normal white vs pure white), and packing (mesh bag vs carton).

Why does the garlic price change during the year? China’s garlic harvest lands in June, and cold-stored stock from Shandong is released steadily through the rest of the year. Prices typically firm up ahead of the Ramadan buying season, when importers in Saudi Arabia, Egypt and Bangladesh book containers early to lock the price. Buying before the peak window is the simplest way to save 5–10% on a full-container load.

Do you quote CIF, not just FOB? Yes — we quote FOB Qingdao, CIF to your port, or CFR. Many buyers in Kenya, Brazil and the UAE ask for landed-cost estimates including freight and insurance. Send us your destination port and size requirement and you will have a firm per-ton quote within 24 hours.

What is the garlic price per kg, and how does it relate to the price per ton? Buyers often ask for both the China garlic price per ton and a per-kilo figure. As a quick guide, divide the FOB Qingdao USD/MT number by 1,000 (so $800/MT ≈ $0.80/kg at origin), then add cold-chain freight, insurance and destination charges for a landed CIF price. Whether you search "fresh garlic price China", "Chinese garlic price" or "garlic price in China today", the figure tracks the June harvest and the release of cold-stored stock — so always request a live quote instead of relying on a published index.
