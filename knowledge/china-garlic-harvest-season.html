China Garlic Harvest Season: When and How to Source Fresh Garlic

Understanding China's garlic growing cycle helps importers time purchases for the best price and quality, from October planting through June harvest.

Quick Answer: Garlic in China is harvested in June, with the main harvest season running from June to August. Prices are lowest in July-August (fresh harvest) and highest in December-February (cold storage + holiday demand). The best time to buy is July-August, when you can save 15-30% compared to winter prices.

The Garlic Growing Cycle in China

Chinese garlic follows an annual growing cycle that begins with planting in autumn and ends with harvest in early summer. Understanding this cycle is essential for importers, as the timing of your purchase relative to the harvest season significantly impacts both price and quality.

Month Stage Market Impact
October Planting season begins Previous year's garlic still in cold storage
November - February Winter growth (dormancy to active) Cold storage prices peak
March - April Stem and bulb development Prices remain high, stock depleting
May Garlic scapes harvested New crop expected soon, prices may dip
June Main harvest season begins Fresh garlic enters market, prices drop
July - August Curing, sorting, cold storage loading Lowest prices of the year
September Cold storage garlic begins selling Prices start gradual climb

When Is the Best Time to Buy Garlic from China?

The optimal purchasing window depends on your priorities:

  • Best price: July to August — Fresh garlic has just been harvested and cured. Supply is abundant, and prices are at their annual low. This is the ideal time to place large orders for cold storage stocking.
  • Best quality: August to October — Garlic has been properly cured and sorted. Cold-stored garlic from this period maintains excellent quality for 6-9 months.
  • Best for immediate delivery: Year-round — Reputable suppliers with cold storage facilities can deliver year-round, but prices will be higher outside the harvest season.

How Weather Affects Harvest Timing and Quality

Garlic harvest timing can shift by 1-2 weeks depending on weather conditions during the growing season. A warm spring can accelerate development, leading to an earlier harvest in late May. Conversely, a cold spring may delay harvest until mid-June. Extreme weather events such as late frosts, heavy rain, or drought can affect both yield and quality:

  • Late frost can damage young shoots, reducing yield and bulb size.
  • Excessive rain during harvest can lead to rotting and quality issues, increasing the percentage of rejected bulbs.
  • Drought can reduce bulb size, shifting the size distribution toward smaller grades.
  • Ideal conditions — cool winter, moderate spring rainfall, dry harvest weather — produce the best quality and yield.

New Crop vs Cold Storage Garlic

Importers should understand the difference between new crop garlic and cold storage garlic:

  • New crop garlic (June-September) is freshly harvested. It has higher moisture content, looser skin, and a more vibrant flavor. Must be properly cured (dried) for 2-3 weeks before shipping.
  • Cold storage garlic (October-May) has been stored at -1 to -3 degrees Celsius. It has lower moisture content, tighter skin, and is better suited for long-distance shipping. However, storage costs are embedded in the price.

For most export shipments, cold storage garlic is preferred because it ships better and has a longer remaining shelf life at destination. For more details, see our Fresh Garlic Storage Guide.

Planning Your Annual Purchasing Calendar

Smart importers align their purchasing with the harvest cycle to optimize both cost and quality. Here is a recommended annual planning calendar:

  • April-May: Contact suppliers to discuss new crop expectations, get preliminary quotes.
  • June-July: Place orders for fresh harvest garlic at the best prices of the year.
  • August-September: Receive shipments, plan for holiday season demand (Ramadan, Chinese New Year).
  • October-November: Place follow-up orders before winter price increases.
  • December-March: Minimize purchases (highest prices); rely on previously stocked inventory.

Frequently Asked Questions

What month is garlic harvested in China?
Garlic is harvested in China starting in late May to early June, with the main harvest period running through July. In Shandong Province, the primary garlic-producing region, harvest typically peaks in mid-June. After harvest, garlic is cured (dried) for 2-3 weeks before being moved to cold storage or shipped.
Can I buy fresh garlic from China year-round?
Yes, garlic is available year-round from China thanks to cold storage facilities. Garlic harvested in June-July is stored at -1 to -3 degrees Celsius and can maintain export quality for 6-9 months. However, prices are 15-30% higher during the off-season (December to May) due to accumulated storage costs.
How does weather affect garlic harvest in China?
Weather conditions during the growing season (October to May) significantly impact garlic yield and quality. Late frosts can damage young shoots, excessive rain during harvest can cause rot, and drought can reduce bulb size. A warm spring may accelerate harvest by 1-2 weeks, while a cold spring may delay it.
